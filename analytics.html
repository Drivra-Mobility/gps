GPS anomalies

Internal consistency checks on the position stream — no external data needed. A "GPS jump" is a physically implausible speed between two readings; "frozen while moving" is a vehicle reporting speed but not actually changing position. Either can mean a bad GPS fix, a spoofed feed, or a device fault — treat as a starting point for investigation, not a verdict.

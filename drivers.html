Vehicle ↔ driver mapping

Phone must be in +977XXXXXXXXXX format to match Yango's own records. A vehicle's driver history is preserved — reassigning opens a new record rather than overwriting the old one, so past movement is still checked against whoever was actually responsible for it at the time. See Misuse review for whether movement is backed by an actual Yango ride.

Unmapped vehicles whose GPS-reported plate matches a driver's vehicle in Yango's own records show a "Suggested" name/phone, pre-filled into the form when you click Review suggestion — check it before saving, it's a starting point from matching plate numbers, not a confirmed assignment.

Why this still needs a human, not just a plate match: a suggestion is only as good as the GPS device's own reported plate, which is entered by hand in the tracker vendor's system and can be wrong or stale — the "IMEI"/"Last heard" columns exist because the same plate sometimes appears on two different trackers (an old unit that was swapped out but never removed still shows the same plate label, frozen at its last reading). Saving here also opens a new dated record rather than overwriting the old one, which a plate match alone can't do — misuse detection needs to know who was actually driving on a given past date, not just who matches right now.

Vehicle type & fuel

Neither GPS provider reports fuel type — electric vs petrol is registered here, by hand, per vehicle. A vehicle with no row here yet falls back to a rough guess from its name/plate text on the map (see VEHICLE_TYPE_RULES in config.js), with no fuel-type marker at all.
